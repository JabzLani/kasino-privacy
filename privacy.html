Kasino Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Kasino is a South African card game. It has no adverts, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no accounts. This policy explains the small amount of information the game handles.

Who is responsible

Kasino is published by its independent developer. Privacy contact: jabulanimlaudzi@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your device

Player names you create, your win streaks and leaderboard, match replays and your settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes them.

Information used for online play

This applies only when you choose Online and join a room.

InformationWhy it is neededHow long it is kept
The display name you typeShown to the other players in your roomWhile the room is open. Rooms are held in memory and are never saved
A random code created on your deviceLets you rejoin your seat if your connection drops. It does not identify you and is not linked to your name or device detailsSent to the server while you are in a room. It stays on your device until you clear the app's data
Your moves and cards in the gameNeeded to run the gameWhile the room is open
Your IP addressNeeded for any internet connection. Our hosting provider, Render, may record it in its routine server logsKept by Render according to its own policy

Display names are visible to the other players in your room, so please don't use your full name or other personal details.

Sharing

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. Online games run on servers provided by Render, which processes this information on our behalf.

Security

Online play uses encrypted connections (HTTPS and secure WebSockets).

Deleting your information

There are no accounts to delete. Room information disappears when a room closes. To remove what is saved on your device, clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Children

Kasino is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
